1. Strengthened business decision-making through actionable recommendations.
2. Deepened validation of major findings.
3. Improved the Power BI dashboard for management use.
4. Documented KPI calculations and business purposes.

## Key Findings

- The top 10% of transactions accounted for 56.15% of total transaction value.
- International transactions had an observed risk-review rate of 36.88%, compared with 18.88% for domestic transactions.
- Mobile App was the highest-volume channel across all customer segments.
- Customer transaction frequency and total customer value showed a moderate positive association.
- Digital engagement showed only a very weak relationship with transaction activity.
