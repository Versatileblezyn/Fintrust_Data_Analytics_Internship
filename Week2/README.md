# FinTrust Week 2 – Data Analytics

## Overview

Week 2 focused on analysing, preparing, developing, testing, and documenting the FinTrust Digital Bank data analytics project.

The work built on the Week 1 business questions and KPI definitions and focused on turning the available customer and transaction data into meaningful business insights.

## Key Findings

1. Everyday customers represented the largest customer segment, with 711 customers (47.4%).
2. Transfers generated the highest transaction volume, with 3,549 transactions (29.58%), and the highest total transaction value of approximately ₦237.92 million.
3. Transaction outcomes varied by transaction type, with Bill Payment recording the highest failure rate at 5.83%.
4. International transactions had a higher observed risk-review rate (36.88%) than domestic transactions (18.88%).
5. Risk-review rates varied more across transaction types than across customer segments.

> Note: Risk_Review_Flag is a synthetic review indicator in the provided dataset and should not be interpreted as a real fraud or banking-risk decision.
